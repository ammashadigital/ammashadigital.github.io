How the bot works:

Real phone calls: to call real numbers (Twilio) the bot needs a phone number and a calling account — that is a small paid setup. This demo lets you test every reply first, then we connect it to real calls when you say so. The bot never invents prices or phone numbers — where a price is not verified it says “price on call”.
